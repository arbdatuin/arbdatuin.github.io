Digital Document Archiving

Digitization of hard-copy sales invoices for Finance – Credit and Collection.

Docukit / Doculink

Business context

Finance – Credit and Collection relied on hard-copy sales invoices. The project introduced a digital document archiving approach using Docukit / Doculink.

Scope and responsibilities

  • Implemented data archiving for the Finance – Credit and Collection team.
  • Digitized hard-copy sales invoices.

Conceptual workflow

Illustrative workflow based on résumé descriptions; not an actual production diagram.

Business value

A digital record format supports document retrieval and reduces reliance on physical originals for day-to-day reference. Retrieval time and storage savings were not measured in the supplied record.

Evidence to add when approved
